What C.O.S.M.O is

An AI cyber threat intelligence platform that runs on your server, with an AI that answers to your team.

C.O.S.M.O takes in your alerts, logs, documents and threat intelligence, then triages, investigates, hunts, responds and predicts, with an assistant at every step. It runs entirely on hardware you control. Nothing leaves your network, and nothing changes until a person approves.

Security

A security tool should be the hardest thing on your network to fool.

C.O.S.M.O processes alerts, dark web posts and uploaded files, all of which an attacker can write. It treats every one as data and never as an instruction. Nothing changes on your network without an analyst’s approval, and every action is recorded in a log that shows any later edit.

  1. 1

    Attackers can plant text

    Alert data, dark web posts and uploaded files can hide instructions meant to trick an AI.

  2. 2

    C.O.S.M.O reads it, never obeys it

    A hidden instruction is shown to you as part of the file. It is never followed, and the file is never run.

  3. 3

    A person approves every change

    The AI can suggest a block or a closed case. Nothing on your network changes until an analyst says yes.

  4. 4

    The log shows any edit

    Every action is written to a record where each entry is sealed to the one before it, so a quiet edit shows.
